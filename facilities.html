Intensive Care Unit (ICU)24×7

30-bed comprehensive critical care unit comprising 15 ICCU beds, 7 MICU beds, 7 SICU beds and 1 isolation bed with ventilators and central monitoring.

What's included

  • 15 ICCU beds dedicated to acute cardiac & coronary emergencies
  • 7 M.I.C.U. beds for severe medical illnesses & multi-organ care
  • 7 S.I.C.U. beds for major surgical & trauma recovery
  • 1 dedicated Isolation ICU bed with glass enclosure
  • Invasive and non-invasive ventilators
  • Multipara central monitoring, ECG, defibrillators & crash cart
  • Central oxygen and suction at every single bed
  • Bedside 2D Echo, sonography and portable X-ray
  • Bedside hemodialysis inside the ICU

Who it's for

Patients who need continuous monitoring or life support: severe infection, heart attack, breathing failure, poisoning, or recovery after major surgery.

Isolation ICU24×7

A dedicated 1-bed glass-walled, separately entered isolation ICU room protecting contagious patients and hospital surroundings.

  • 1 dedicated glass-door isolation bed separate from the main ICU
  • Full ICU bed with central monitor, oxygen and suction
  • Bedside ultrasound and ventilator support
  • Dedicated barrier nursing & infection-control protocol

Nursing Station & Central Monitoring24×7

Experienced ICU nurses watch every bed's vitals on a central screen and respond within moments.

  • Central monitor showing every ICU bed's vitals
  • Qualified ICU nursing staff on every shift
  • Emergency crash cart and medicine trolley on the floor
  • Duty roster board for clear handovers

Emergency & Trauma24×7

A casualty desk with an immediate doctor response for accidents, poisoning, snakebite, burns, stroke and high fever.

What's included

  • Road accident and multiple-injury (polytrauma) care
  • Snakebite protocol with anti-snake venom
  • Poisoning management and antidotes
  • Acute stroke and brain haemorrhage care
  • Burns, electric shock and heat stroke
  • Triage on arrival with a doctor on the floor 24x7

Who it's for

Anyone who needs urgent medical attention. No appointment needed: come straight in or call ahead.

24x7 Emergency Ambulance Service

Dedicated 24x7 patient pickup and transfer ambulance fitted with continuous medical oxygen, stretcher, emergency first-aid kit, and trained paramedical staff.

  • Rapid response patient transport across Modasa and Aravalli district
  • Piped medical oxygen cylinder and emergency suction apparatus
  • Emergency folding stretcher and wheelchair boarding facility
  • Emergency resuscitation kit, splints, and vital medicines on board
  • Direct 24x7 emergency helpline dispatch: 95120 45641

24x7 Dialysis Unit

Emergency and scheduled haemodialysis, supported by high-grade dialysis machines and a dedicated RO water purification plant.

What's included

  • Hemodialysis machines with dedicated RO water treatment plant
  • Full inpatient dialysis support for the 41-bed facility
  • Emergency dialysis for acute kidney failure & poisonings
  • Scheduled maintenance dialysis sessions
  • ICU bedside dialysis for critically ill patients
  • Separate machine protocol for seropositive patients

Who it's for

Patients with kidney failure: both regular dialysis patients and emergencies.

In-House CT Scan24×7

Modasa CT Scan Centre on the same premises with high-precision gantry and technician workstation, requiring zero transfer delay.

Representative image

What's included

  • Modasa CT Scan machine (gantry) located in-house
  • Dedicated technician workstation for rapid slice reconstruction
  • Brain and head CT for stroke and trauma hemorrhage
  • HRCT chest for pneumonia and pulmonary evaluation
  • Abdomen, pelvis, spine and musculoskeletal CT
  • Priority 24x7 reporting for emergency cases

Who it's for

Head injury, suspected stroke, chest infections, abdominal pain, and trauma cases that need fast imaging.

Digital X-Ray, 2D Echo & Sonography24×7

Heart ultrasound, abdominal sonography and digital X-ray, including portable machines that come to the bed.

  • 2D Echocardiography with colour Doppler
  • Abdominal and emergency (FAST) sonography
  • Digital X-ray and mobile bedside X-ray
  • Heart failure and valve function assessment

24x7 Pathology Laboratory

Automated blood testing machines, cell counter and sample counter operating 24 hours so treatment is never delayed.

What's included

  • Automated blood testing machines & automated cell counter
  • Dedicated sample counter and barcode-managed workflows
  • Arterial blood gas (ABG) analysis for ICU patients
  • Cardiac troponin (rapid heart attack marker)
  • Automated biochemistry, haematology & electrolyte panels
  • Dengue, malaria, typhoid panels and platelet counts

Who it's for

Routine check-ups, fever work-ups, and emergency tests for ICU patients.

Lung Function & Bronchoscopy Lab

Specialist lung testing led by the chest physician: bronchoscopy, spirometry and sleep studies.

Representative image

What's included

  • Fiberoptic video bronchoscopy (દુરબીન દ્વારા ફેફસાની તપાસ)

Physiotherapy Department

Rehabilitation that helps patients get moving again after surgery, a stroke, a fracture or a long ICU stay.

  • Post-surgery and post-fracture rehabilitation
  • Stroke (paralysis) recovery exercises
  • Chest physiotherapy for lung patients
  • Mobilisation of ICU and bed-bound patients

Modular Operation Theatre24×7

Sterile modular theatres with laminar airflow, LED surgical lights and a full anaesthesia workstation.

What's included

  • Laminar airflow ceiling for infection control
  • IITV (live X-ray) for orthopaedic surgery
  • Anaesthesia workstation and cardiac monitors
  • General, emergency and high-risk surgery
  • Plastic surgery

Who it's for

Planned and emergency operations, including fracture fixation, general and abdominal surgery.

Laparoscopy, Endoscopy & Specialty Surgery

Keyhole surgery and camera procedures, plus neuro/spine and maxillofacial (dental) surgical wings.

  • Laparoscopic (keyhole) surgery
  • Endoscopy for stomach and intestine
  • Gastroenterology & hepatology procedures
  • Neuro and spine surgery
  • Maxillofacial (jaw & dental) surgery

Patients who prefer privacy during their stay.
