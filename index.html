Patient & Visitor Guide

Essential information before you visit

Clear clinical protocols designed to save critical minutes and ensure seamless medical care.

Emergency Protocol

No prior appointment needed for acute emergencies. Walk directly into the casualty bay on 4th Floor, City Centre or call ahead so our ICU and trauma team is alerted before arrival.

OPD Consultations

Senior visiting MD consultants consult Monday to Saturday in designated morning and evening sessions. Reserve a 30-minute advance slot to avoid prolonged waiting room delays.

Morning: 09:00 - 01:30 | Evening: 04:30 - 08:00

What to Bring

Please carry your past hospital file, recent blood test reports, prior ECG or CT scans, and a list of ongoing medications so the doctor can evaluate treatment continuity accurately.

FAQ

Questions patients often ask

How does advance slot booking work?
Choose a doctor, pick any day in the coming week and a 30-minute morning or evening slot. Your request opens in WhatsApp addressed to the hospital reception, and you get a booking ID (e.g. PLS-920101) and a printable slip. Reception confirms the slot on WhatsApp.
Do I need an appointment for an emergency?
No. The emergency desk, ICU, CT scan, lab, pharmacy and dialysis run 24x7. Come straight to the 4th floor, City Centre, or call ahead so the team is ready.
Can I check or cancel my booking?
Yes. Use “My Booking” and search with your mobile number or booking ID. It lists bookings made from this phone or computer; you can reopen the slip, or cancel, which prepares a WhatsApp message to reception so the slot can be released.
What should I bring to my appointment?
Please bring your hospital file and all previous medical reports, any recent lab tests and prescriptions, and a photo ID.
Where is Pulse Hospital located?
4th Floor, A-block, City Centre, Shamlaji Road, Modasa, Arvalli - 383315. Lift access, wheelchair assistance and ample parking are available.
