Our story

Caring for life, in every moment

Patients who once had to rush 100+ kilometres to Ahmedabad for ventilator support, emergency dialysis or a CT scan can now receive that care right here in Modasa.

Our ICU is staffed 24x7 by qualified doctors and experienced critical-care nurses, with invasive and non-invasive ventilators, bedside sonography, 2D Echo and central monitoring.

What we stand for

Four promises to every patient

Always open

Emergency, ICU, lab, pharmacy and dialysis never close, including at night and on Sundays.

Critical care nearby

Ventilators, CT and dialysis in Modasa, so families don’t have to travel 100+ km in a crisis.

Senior doctors

Experienced MD physicians and a chest specialist, with a medical officer in the ICU around the clock.

Safe, clean spaces

Laminar-airflow theatres, an isolation ICU and strict infection control.
